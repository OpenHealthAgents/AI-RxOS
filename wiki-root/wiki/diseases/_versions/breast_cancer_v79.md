## Primary Summary
Trastuzumab Deruxtecan-Associated Pneumonitis in Non-Breast Solid Tumors: A Retrospective Cohort Study.. INTRODUCTION: Trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) has transformed treatment for HER2-expressing malignancies, yet pneumonitis remains a potentially fatal toxicity. Most pneumonitis data derive from breast cancer cohorts, but risk factors and outcomes in non-breast solid organ tumors are poorly characterized.
