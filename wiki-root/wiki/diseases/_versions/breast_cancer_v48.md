Lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) have emerged as a key delivery platform for mRNA therapeutics, as demonstrated by the clinical success of mRNA vaccines against SARS-CoV-2. LNPs are now being investigated for various applications, such as cancer immunotherapy and the treatment of genetic disorders.
