## Primary Summary
Preclinical evaluation of trastuzumab deruxtecan in a new HER2+ leptomeningeal colonization model.. Leptomeningeal metastases are devastating complications of advanced HER2+ breast cancer, with limited therapeutic options. We developed a xenograft model of human HER2+ breast cancer cell line JIMT1-BR3-LM4 leptomeningeal colonization by four iterative cycles of intrathecal injection.
